## How to use this log

For each observation, capture: the date, what happened, and which bucket it falls in.
Buckets to track (from the MVP plan):

- **Bugs** — something broke or gave a wrong result.
- **Friction** — it worked but was slow, awkward, or annoying.
- **Missing functionality** — something needed that isn't there.
- **Frequent AI questions** — what you actually ask "Ask OBSIDIAN" most.
- **Data-entry pain points** — where logging trips/expenses feels heavy.
- **Valuable insights** — insights that genuinely changed a decision.
